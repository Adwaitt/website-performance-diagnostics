## 1. Executive Diagnostic Summary

A multi-stream diagnostic ingestion was performed across **Google Analytics**, **Microsoft Clarity**, and **Edge Ingress Server Logs** for the observation window on `2026-10-18`.

```
           TRAFFIC & INGRESS                      BEHAVIORAL ENGAGEMENT                      CONVERSION
 [ 100% HTTP 200 OK | p95 < 38ms ]  ───►  [ High Scroll (72–85%) | High Dwell (165–328s) ]  ───►  [ 0% Add-to-Cart (ATC) ]
      Infrastructure Healthy                        Deep User Consideration                            Pre-Cart Churn
```

* **Infrastructure & Platform Health:** **Nominal (P0 Zero Outages).** Edge latency averaged $35\text{ ms}$, upstream response times were $\le 36\text{ ms}$, HTTP status codes were uniformly `200 OK`, and zero client-side JavaScript or network exceptions were logged.
* **Conversion Blockers:** The conversion funnel suffers an absolute drop-off between the **Product Detail Page (PDP) evaluation phase** and **Add-to-Cart (ATC) trigger**. Users exhibit strong intent, long dwell times ($>2.5$ to $5.5$ minutes), deep page penetration ($72\%\text{--}85\%$ scroll depth), and zero rage/dead clicks. 
* **Primary Causal Drivers:**
  1. **PDP Decision Paralysis / Spec Friction (P1):** Users inspect deep technical details (accordion expansions, sizing tables) and drop off without committing, pointing to ambiguous product specs, lack of sizing confidence, or missing inline social proof/guarantees.
  2. **Geo-Specific / International Commerce Friction (P2):** International traffic (e.g., India, UK) landing on product pages without localized currency, dynamic duties, or clear local fulfillment timelines.

## 5. UX & Behavioral Friction Points

### 1. PDP "Decision Paralysis" & Friction (Merino Wool Pullover)
* **Behavior:** The user filtered for "Size: Large", reached the PDP, and opened the size chart.
* **UX Flaw:** If a user filters by size on a collection page and views a product, sizing data should be pre-selected and contextually reinforced. The necessity to review the generic size chart implies ambiguity (e.g., standard sizing vs. slim fit, absence of imperial/metric conversion for Indian/UK shoppers, or lack of "Model is 6'2 wearing L" guidance).

### 2. High Consideration Without Conversion Reassurance (Noise-Canceling Headphones)
* **Behavior:** 328 seconds spent reading specifications and reviews on an iOS device.
* **UX Flaw:**
  * **Sticky CTA Absence:** On mobile Safari, after scrolling 85% down into reviews, the `#add-to-cart` button is scrolled out of viewport. Without a persistent sticky bottom drawer with an active ATC button, re-scroll fatigue causes session abandonment.
  * **Missing Friction Reducers:** High-ticket electronics require immediate reassurance at the specification level (warranty badges, battery replacement guarantees, return policy summaries, and financing options like Klarna/Clearpay).

### 3. Internationalization & Geo-Targeting Gaps
* Traffic from **London, GB** (`82.165.197.1`) and **Mumbai, IN** (`103.21.124.55`) had no evidence of geo-localized storefronts (currency selectors, localized shipping estimates, or import tax transparency). Cross-border visitors reviewing checkout-adjacent items drop off when total landed cost is ambiguous prior to checkout.
